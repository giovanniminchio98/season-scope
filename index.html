Dynamic membership: the top 50 / 100 companies by market cap (refreshed nightly) plus forex, commodities & indices — then ranked by an opportunity / timing grade from seasonal edge (months ahead historically strong), momentum (3/6-mo trend + RSI), value / entry (drawdown from highs, 52-wk range position, vs 200-day trend), plus an analyst-target tilt when the nightly snapshot is available. Asset Rating · Quality vs Opportunity
—
Quality · —
—
Opportunity now · —
Quality = how steady this asset has been over the years (momentum/CAGR, trend consistency, drawdown control, volatility, seasonal reliability) — a long-term grade. Opportunity now = the same timing score used on the Discover tab (upcoming-month seasonal edge, recent momentum, value/entry). They differ on purpose: a stable name can be high Quality but low Opportunity if it's not an attractive entry today. SeasonScope's own metrics — not a Morningstar rating, not investment advice.
Valuation & Entry · Price-Based Signal
—
computed from price
Two layers: when the nightly snapshot is available, analyst data from Yahoo Finance (mean price target → over/under-valued, rating, buy/hold/sell consensus, refreshed by the GitHub Action each night). Below that, a technical read computed from price — drawdown from highs, 52-week range position, distance from the 200-day trend, RSI. Snapshot data is up to a day old. Not investment advice.
Intra-Month · Day of Week
Average daily % change by weekday in the selected window. Surfaces patterns like Monday weakness / mid-week strength. Magnitudes are small — read the relative shape and win-rate.
Intra-Month · Week of Month
Average daily % change bucketed by day-of-month. The first/last buckets capture the classic turn-of-the-month effect (fund inflows, paycheck cycles).
